The challenge

Entrepreneurship learning is often:

  • passive
  • fragmented
  • difficult to sustain consistently

The opportunity:

  • support both reading and listening behaviors
  • make learning feel flexible across contexts
  • encourage users to return daily

Reading + Audio

Continuity fractures when people jump between reading, listening, and short sessions—the shell had to feel like one product, not three modes bolted together.

Design 1

Navigation & player

Navigation and player chrome so depth stayed high but entry points stayed obvious.

Retention

Engagement drops when long chapters are easy to skim and forget—interaction had to stay light, in-column, and worth coming back to.

Encourage active engagement and recall without breaking immersion.

Motivation

Behavior won’t move on heavy gamification—people need momentum signals that don’t hijack attention from the actual lesson.

Design 5

Milestones, streaks, and onboarding loops as glanceable rewards for daily return.

Brand

Build & ship

Built in a fast-moving startup environment with tight design–engineering iteration loops.

  • Shipped MVP in incremental releases instead of fully spec’d builds
  • Worked directly with engineers in rapid design → build → refine cycles ⚙️
  • Got quick feedback from test users in parallel and iterated as we shipped
  • Simplified early concepts (navigation, annotations, gamification) to keep core learning flow sharp
  • Evolved the design system alongside product build — nothing was fully “final,” everything stayed in motion 🔄

Key takeaway

Optimized for speed, clarity, and iteration — not perfection.
